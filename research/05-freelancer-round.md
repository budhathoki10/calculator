**No freelancer idea scores 10/10, and none reaches the 6.0 "test first" bar.** The best *legal* freelancer idea found scores **4.90**. The idea that had the most demand evidence, Upwork job alerts, is now ruled out by Upwork's own rules.

## New evidence this round

- **Upwork bans job-watcher tools.** Upwork's help article "Use bots and other automation properly" treats any script, program or browser extension that acts faster or more often than a person could as automation. Its examples include "job alert or watcher tools that run searches" and auto-refresh tools; unauthorised use can lead to warnings or bans [SE support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/43342677368467]. On extensions: "you are responsible for making sure the extension you're using does not use Upwork's API in any way that violates our Terms of Service", and violations "can result in temporary or permanent account suspensions" [SE support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/17652357074451]. [I] A job-alert product would put its users' Upwork accounts at risk, which breaks your ethics rule.
- **Contra** is free for freelancers. It charges clients $29 per contract or per month, and Contra Pro for freelancers costs $29/mo. Contra says it "does not make money by taking a commission" [SE contra.com/faq/clients/general; contra.com/blog/payments (Feb 2026)]. Whether Contra pays out to Nepal is **unknown**: no Stripe-based payout list found includes Nepal [SE help.dribbble.com; get.bandcamp.help].
- **Payoneer request-a-payment:** card up to 3.99% + $0.49; ACH from US payers 1%; no fee when receiving in your local-currency account, otherwise 1% [SE payoneer.com/fees]. [I] Direct-client payment for Nepali freelancers works through Payoneer without any product holding money.
- **Payment trust gap (Pakistan, old):** "many clients are reluctant to do business if you don't have a PayPal account" [SE dawn.com/news/amp/1489894, 2019, old].
- **Testimonial tools:** Senja free for 15 testimonials, then about $29/mo; Testimonial.to about $50/mo for paid tiers [SE senja.io blog; checkthat.ai; competitor-written].
- **LinkedIn content tools:** Taplio $39 / $69 / $199 a month (vendor page); AuthoredUp $12–$19.95 [SE taplio.com/blog/taplio-pricing; theguidex.com]. One comparison flags Taplio's engagement automation as carrying "documented LinkedIn account suspension risk" [SE thegtmdirectory.com].
- Terms for public job feeds (Remotive API, We Work Remotely RSS, Hacker News) were **not found** (unknown).

## What this means

[I] For freelancers, the job "get me clients" is held by marketplaces that forbid outside automation (Upwork), by free platforms (Contra, LinkedIn, job boards) and by funded tools (HoneyBook, Taplio). The research found no gap that a solo founder can fill legally and defend.

The most promising unexplored direction is still the one named in `04`: **interview 20 Nepali/South Asian freelancers** about the last time they lost money or a client. Their own words are missing from this research (Reddit and local forums were unreachable). This is the most likely place for a better idea.
