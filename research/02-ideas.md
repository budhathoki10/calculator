### 1. Upwork Job Radar (P2, P4) — tool
- **Target user:** Upwork freelancers, especially in South Asia.
- **Job:** Find the right new Upwork jobs within minutes of posting, score them for fit and client quality, and alert the freelancer.
- **How it gets customers:** Earlier, better-targeted proposals mean more views and interviews.
- **How I charge:** $9/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Vollna $4–$49/mo (directory) or $24/$62 (competitor's figure) [SE softwaresuggest.com/vollna; uphunt.io]. GigRadar $129/mo plus $20/mo Upwork Agency Plus [SE gigradar.io/pricing]. UpHunt, UpworkAlerts. Upwork's own saved searches and notifications (free) [SE gigradar.io/blog/upwork-rss-feed]. Upwork's built-in Uma AI drafts proposals ("Freelancer Plus gives full access") [SE support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/22983791592595].

### 2. Marketplace profile and proposal coach (P2, P4) — tool
- **Target user:** New Fiverr and Upwork sellers with few reviews.
- **Job:** Audit the gig or profile and rewrite proposals against what wins in their category.
- **How it gets customers:** Higher view-to-hire conversion.
- **How I charge:** $12/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Upwork Uma (built in); many "Upwork proposal" Gumroad templates [SE gumroad search results]; aiproposer.com; general chatbots (free tiers).

### 3. Public buying-intent monitor (P2) — tool
- **Target user:** Freelancers and consultants.
- **Job:** Watch public forums and social posts where people ask for a service ("looking for a Shopify dev") and alert the user so they can reply helpfully in public.
- **How it gets customers:** Turns public requests into warm conversations, one to one, with no bulk messaging.
- **How I charge:** $15/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** F5Bot (free; Reddit and Hacker News) [SE stork.ai/alternatives/f5bot]; Syften (price unknown). GummySearch closed because of "inability to reach a commercial license agreement with Reddit's Data API" [SE gummysearch.com/closing-time, signups closed 30 Nov 2025]. X API is pay-per-use, about $0.005 per post read [SE techcrunch.com 21 Oct 2025; roboin.io Feb 2026].

### 4. Solo review-and-referral engine for local service providers (P3, P1) — tool
- **Target user:** One-person local businesses: cleaners, handymen, trainers, beauticians, photographers, tutors.
- **Job:** After each job, prompt the provider to send a pre-written, personal review request and, a few weeks later, a referral ask and a rebooking nudge. Messages go *from the provider's own phone* (WhatsApp, SMS or email deep links), so there is no bulk sending and no per-message API cost. It tracks who referred whom.
- **How it gets customers:** More recent Google reviews (a ranking and conversion factor) and more referred customers.
- **How I charge:** $12/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** NiceJob $75/mo [SE get.nicejob.com/pricing]; ReviewNudger $29/mo [SE getapp listing]; Podium $399+/mo on 12-month contracts [SE pabau.com]; Birdeye (price unknown). Field-service suites with built-in review requests: Jobber, Housecall Pro (prices not collected). Free substitutes: Google's free review link plus phone reminders.

### 5. Past-client reactivation and referral system for freelancers ("client garden") (P3, P2) — tool
- **Target user:** Freelancers with direct clients: designers, developers, writers, virtual assistants, consultants.
- **Job:** Keep a light record of past clients, remind the freelancer at sensible intervals to check in personally (project anniversary, launch follow-up, seasonal needs), draft the message, ask for referrals at the right moment, and track referral sources.
- **How it gets customers:** Repeat work and referrals, the channel that produces 62–75% of freelance work in surveys.
- **How I charge:** $10/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Dex $12/mo, folk $24–30 per user per month [SE happenstance.ai; capterra]; HoneyBook about $29–36/mo; Dubsado about $20–28/mo [SE prospeo.io; assembly.com]. Spreadsheets and calendar reminders (free).

### 6. Independent-tutor growth kit (P3, P4) — niche (one profession)
- **Target user:** Tutors who teach some or all students independently (online or in person), including tutors trying to depend less on Preply or Wyzant *for new students*.
- **Job:** Monthly parent progress notes (drafted, then sent by the tutor), timed review and referral asks to parents, a simple referral reward the tutor controls, a public tutor page with verified reviews, and a waitlist.
- **How it gets customers:** Parent-to-parent referrals and visible reviews. Retention also rises because parents see progress.
- **How I charge:** $12/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** TutorBird $14.95/mo [SE toolradar.com]; Teachworks $16.49/mo plus $0.32 per lesson [SE teachngo.com]; both are admin tools, not acquisition tools. Wyzant lets tutors bring outside students and keep 100% [SE support.wyzant.com]. Free substitutes: WhatsApp and Google Business Profile.
- **Note:** The kit must **not** help move students who were met on Preply or Wyzant off those platforms, since that breaks their terms.

### 7. Rebooking engine for portrait and family photographers (P1, P3) — niche (one profession)
- **Target user:** Portrait, family and newborn photographers.
- **Job:** Remind past clients of natural rebooking moments (birthdays, anniversaries, seasonal mini-sessions) through personal messages, and run referral asks.
- **How it gets customers:** Repeat sessions and referrals instead of The Knot-style paid listings.
- **How I charge:** $15/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Pixieset Studio Manager free tier, then $10–$50/mo [SE costbench.com]; Studio Ninja $24–$53/mo [SE costbench.com]; HoneyBook and Dubsado.

### 8. Google Business Profile autopilot for solo providers (P3) — tool
- **Target user:** Solo local providers.
- **Job:** Weekly profile posts, review replies, Q&A and photo prompts.
- **How it gets customers:** Better local ranking and conversion (Google: review count and score "factor into local search ranking" [SE support.google.com/business/answer/7091]).
- **How I charge:** $15/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** BrightLocal from $39/mo, Localo from $69/mo, Paige $99/mo [SE Capterra / Software Advice 2026]. The Google Business Profile app itself is free.

### 9. Missed-call text-back / AI receptionist for solo trades (P1) — tool
- **Target user:** Solo tradespeople who miss calls while on jobs.
- **Job:** Instantly text back missed callers and capture the job details.
- **How it gets customers:** Fewer lost inbound leads.
- **How I charge:** $25/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** $29–$300/mo range; Jobber add-on $29 for 30 conversations; Rosie $49; Goodcall $79 [SE sagnikbhattacharya.com; trillet.ai, vendor-affiliated]. Phone carriers' auto-reply (free).

### 10. Online referral circle for complementary freelancers (P3) — hybrid / network
- **Target user:** Freelancers whose services complement each other (designer + developer + copywriter + photographer).
- **Job:** Small, curated, accountable referral groups (BNI-style but online and cheaper) with a referral log.
- **How it gets customers:** Peer referrals.
- **How I charge:** $15/mo per member.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** BNI about $699/yr, self-reported 27.7 referrals per member per year [SE growwithbni.bni.com]; independent estimate $998–$1,398 in year one plus a $249 application fee [SE diymarketers.com]. Alignable (free tier). Informal Slack and Discord groups (free).

### 11. Paid-lead response accelerator for Thumbtack, Bark and Yelp pros (P1) — hybrid tool on top of marketplaces
- **Target user:** Pros who buy leads.
- **Job:** Respond within minutes with a tailored first message, follow up automatically, and track cost per *won* job by source.
- **How it gets customers:** Higher close rate on leads already paid for. Thumbtack staff advise responding "within the first few minutes" [SE community.thumbtack.com].
- **How I charge:** $29/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Jobber and Housecall Pro already sync Thumbtack leads [SE help.getjobber.com; help.housecallpro.com]; Contractor+; sameday.ai. Thumbtack "only supports one third-party integration at a time" [SE support.contractorplus.app].

### 12. Nepal home-tuition marketplace (P2) — marketplace, one region
- **Target user:** Parents in Kathmandu Valley and home tutors.
- **Job:** Verified tutor matching.
- **How it gets customers:** Parents find tutors through the platform.
- **How I charge:** First-month commission (agency norm elsewhere is about 50% of the first month [SE tutopiya.com, Singapore]), or a tutor subscription of NPR 500/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** TeachMandu, E-Tutor Class, Seekshya, Learnify [SE App Store]; tuition bureaus; Facebook groups.

### 13. Nepal home-services marketplace (P1) — marketplace, one region
- **Target user:** Plumbers, electricians and cleaners in Nepali cities.
- **Job:** Booking marketplace.
- **How it gets customers:** Households book through the app.
- **How I charge:** Commission or lead fee.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Sajilo Sewa, Skill Sewa, Sajilo Mistri, Nirman Sathi [SE onlinekhabar.com].

### 14. Curated South Asian talent marketplace for overseas small businesses (P2, P5) — marketplace
- **Target user:** Vetted Nepali and South Asian freelancers, and small businesses abroad.
- **Job:** Curated matching, cheaper and more trusted than Upwork.
- **How it gets customers:** Inbound overseas buyers.
- **How I charge:** Commission.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Upwork (0–15% fee), Fiverr (20% plus buyer 5.5%), Toptal, Contra, Arc (prices not collected).

### 15. Development-sector consultancy and ToR digest for South Asia (P2, P7) — service-assisted, one region
- **Target user:** Individual consultants (evaluation, research, training, GIS, translation) in Nepal and South Asia.
- **Job:** Hand-curated weekly digest of consultancy terms of reference (ToRs) from UN, INGO, multilateral and government sources, with deadlines and eligibility.
- **How it gets customers:** Consultants find and bid on more relevant assignments.
- **How I charge:** $8/mo, or NPR equivalent.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Devex Pro $399/yr or $59/mo [SE devex.com/pro]; DevelopmentAid (paid; published "21,468 consultancy assignments" in 2025 [SE developmentaid.org]); Impactpool; ReliefWeb and UNjobs (free).

### 16. Sponsor finder for small newsletter, podcast and YouTube creators (P6) — tool
- **Target user:** Creators with 1k–50k audience.
- **Job:** Show which brands sponsor creators in the same niche (from public sponsor reads and ads), with pitch templates.
- **How it gets customers:** Sponsorship deals.
- **How I charge:** $15/mo.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Who Sponsors Stuff (paid; "$100,000 a year" revenue [SE indiehustle.beehiiv.com]); Passionfroot (free for creators, 15% sourcing fee on network deals [SE help.passionfroot.me]); SponsorGap; built-in ad networks such as beehiiv's.

### 17. Done-with-you client-acquisition sprint (P2) — service-assisted
- **Target user:** Freelancers with skills but no pipeline.
- **Job:** Four-week cohort: positioning, offer, portfolio case study, referral and warm-network plan, weekly accountability, with tool support.
- **How it gets customers:** Structured warm outreach and referrals.
- **How I charge:** $99 per cohort plus a $10/mo tool.
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Countless freelance courses and coaches; free YouTube content.

### 18. "Hamro Page": booking, reviews and QR payments page for Nepali solo providers (P3, P5) — tool, one region
- **Target user:** Beauticians, tutors, photographers and repair people in Nepal.
- **Job:** One shareable page with services, reviews, booking requests and the provider's own Fonepay, eSewa or Khalti QR code (money goes straight to the provider, never through me).
- **How it gets customers:** A trust page to share in Facebook, TikTok and Instagram posts, plus a review flywheel.
- **How I charge:** NPR 300–500/mo through Khalti or eSewa (needs a registered business; see `03-validation.md`, Round 4).
- **Competitors and substitutes:** Facebook pages (free), Instagram, TikTok, Linktree (free tier), Google Business Profile (free).

## Ideas rejected before Round 1 under the ethics and law rules

| Idea | Rule broken |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn auto-connect and auto-DM tool | LinkedIn prohibits automation software (User Agreement 8.2) [SE linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387]; also bulk unsolicited DMs |
| "Take your Upwork / Preply / Fiverr clients off-platform" tool | Upwork's 24-month non-circumvention rule, with a "Conversion Fee" to leave [SE community.upwork.com 2022; conductatlas.com]; Preply and Fiverr forbid off-platform dealing |
| Lead-list builder from scraped emails and phone numbers plus cold-email sequencer | Scraping personal data and bulk unsolicited email |
| Escrow or payment-splitting marketplace run from Nepal | Holding or transmitting others' money: NRB licensing for payment-related work [SE pradhanlaw.com, Licensing Policy 2079 (2023)]; Dodo and Paddle list escrow among unsupported businesses [SE docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/merchant-acceptance] |
| Review-generation service with incentives | Google bans incentivised reviews and review gating [SE brightlocal.com; demandhub.co] |
